## Terms and gaps

The two individual seller ads cited above mention one year of warranty and delivery. These are claims about those ads. Global warranty terms, service coverage, delivery areas, costs, timelines, finance terms, return policy and stock counts require owner confirmation. No storewide promotion or arrival date was verified. Recorded prices require reconfirmation before payment.

The [provided Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/telefon28mobile/) could not be independently opened during research. WorldPlaces references a Facebook account, but ownership was only evidenced by that directory. Searches for official BirMarket, Google Maps and TikTok profiles were inconclusive. The address and seller-specific ads were used to distinguish this business from similarly named retailers. An older [phone marketplace profile](https://telefonsatisi.az/shop/telefon28mobile) shows another contact number. The demo uses the number disclosed directly in the Tap.az listing.

Seller photographs and logos require reuse permission. The demo uses illustrative assets. Before a commercial launch, obtain approved model-specific photography, live inventory and pricing, and the owner's signed-off sales and privacy terms.
